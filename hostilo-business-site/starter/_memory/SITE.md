# Firma Przykład: website memory

## Company
Firma Przykład sp. z o.o. designs and manufactures culverts and steel structures for roads and railways (B2B: contractors, road authorities). Tone: factual, formal, "we". No marketing superlatives, no invented numbers.

## Domain and languages
Live: https://example.com (until the custom domain is connected: https://<name>.hostilo.page). Default language: pl (root). Other: en (/en/). EN checked by: export department.

## Structure rules
Header and footer identical on every page of a language (<!-- header --> … <!-- /header -->). One stylesheet /assets/site.css. Menu order: Start/Home, O nas/About, Aktualności/News, Kontakt/Contact. Do not change company data or legal texts without the owner.

## Integrations
Form: Hostilo form (site_token from get_site), thank-you pages above. Analytics: Hostilo statistics only (no cookies, no banner). CRM: none.

## News
About once a month: trade fairs, new products, finished projects. Every post in pl and en, same slug. Photos: WebP via the Hostilo upload link.
